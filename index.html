Family Law Information Center

Family law matters can affect personal relationships, finances, and the care of children. The topics below offer a starting point for understanding questions commonly raised in family law matters.

Divorce and Legal Separation

Divorce generally involves legally ending a marriage. Depending on the circumstances, decisions may be needed about property, debts, support, and parenting arrangements. Legal separation may address similar subjects without ending the marriage. Whether either route fits your circumstances depends on the facts and applicable law.

Child Custody and Visitation

Custody arrangements may cover major decisions for a child as well as where and when the child lives with each parent. A qualified family law professional can explain the legal standards and available procedures relevant to your situation.

Child and Spousal Support

Child support and spousal support questions often depend on finances, family circumstances, and relevant legal rules. An independent provider can discuss what information may be needed to evaluate a particular situation.

Property, Assets and Debts

Some divorces involve questions about shared property, separate assets, business interests, or financial obligations. Organizing financial information can be useful when seeking individualized legal guidance.
